Mobile Photography & Critique

Seeing Santa Rosa Through New Eyes.

This blogsite was developed by six (6) students of the Bachelor of Science in Information Technology (BSIT) 3-2 program at the Polytechnic University of the Philippines, Santa Rosa Campus, as a requirement for the course Art Appreciation, Academic Year 2026-2027. It presents ten (10) original photographs, taken and owned by the developers themselves, captured around Nuvali and the old museum near the Santa Rosa Plaza. Each photograph undergoes an academic evaluation through the stages of Description, Analysis, and Interpretation, and is further classified under one of the four Aesthetic Theories: Imitationalism, Formalism, Emotionalism, and Utilitarianism.
