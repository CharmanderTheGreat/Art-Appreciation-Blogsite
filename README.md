## 📍 Image Documentation & Location Context

All photographic documentations, architectural captures, and on-site subject images featured across this blog were personally captured in and around *Santa Rosa, Laguna*. These photographs highlight local landmarks, public art installations, historical structures, and community aesthetics explored during our fieldwork.

---

## 🎨 Blog Content Overview

* *Visual Arts:* Critical breakdowns of local artworks and classical pieces, highlighting color, composition, and emotional impact.
* *Sculpture & Public Monuments:* Field observations of physical sculptures and civic monuments located around Santa Rosa.
* *Art Critiques & Cultural Reflections:* Individual team essays on the social relevance of art, local culture, and personal interpretations.

---

## 🛠️ Built With

* *HTML5:* Semantic markup structure (all code consolidated in a single index.html).
* *CSS3:* Responsive layout using CSS Grid, Flexbox, and a dark aesthetic theme.
* *JavaScript (Vanilla):* Dynamic client-side category filtering.
* *GitHub Pages:* Static site hosting and deployment.
